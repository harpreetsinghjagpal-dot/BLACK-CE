Canadian Road — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Canadian Road shows black ice risk, 24-hour warnings, official road conditions and live road camera links for highways across Canada. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Your location

  • If you allow location access, the app uses your phone's GPS to show where you are on the map, find the highway you are on, and show the risk for the town ahead.
  • Your location is processed only on your device. The app does not send your location to us or to anyone else, and does not store it.
  • You can turn location off at any time with the GPS button or in your phone's settings. The app still works without it.

Weather and road data

  • Weather comes from Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com). The app asks for the weather at fixed towns along each highway, not at your location.
  • Camera buttons open pictures on official provincial websites (for example 511 Alberta, DriveBC, Ontario 511). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Information stored on your device

  • The app remembers your chosen language on your device. It is not sent anywhere.
  • The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Driver reports and comments

Where driver reports and comments are available, what you submit (your road colour choice, your comment and the time) is visible to other users of the app. Do not include personal information in comments. Reports are kept for a limited time.

On the website, reports and comments are sent anonymously to the public message service ntfy.sh, which keeps them for about 12 hours. The app stores a random device ID in your browser so you can change or remove your own report; no name, email or location is sent.

Children

The app is a road-safety tool for drivers and is not directed at children.

Safety notice

Risk levels are estimates. Black ice is often invisible. Always drive for the conditions and check your provincial 511 service before you travel. Do not use the app while driving; let a passenger use it or pull over safely.
